Delicious Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prep Time: 15 mins

Cook Time: 10 mins

Servings: 12 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cream together butter and sugar until smooth.
  3. Beat in eggs one at a time.
  4. Stir in flour gradually, then fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Bake for 10 minutes until golden brown.
